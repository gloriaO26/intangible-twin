Science layer

The theoretical and empirical grounding behind each of the eight dimensions. Built on peer-reviewed research. Understanding the science helps you interpret what your scores are actually measuring.

Theoretical foundation: Intellectual Capital (IC) × Dynamic Capabilities of the Firm — applied by Oliomogbe (2017) to project-based organisations in construction. IC identifies the stock of intangible assets (human, structural, relational capital). Dynamic Capabilities explain how organisations sense, seize and reconfigure those assets as conditions change. In the AI age, the unit of analysis shifts from role to workflow — and the question becomes: what is AI doing to each layer of organisational capital, visibly and invisibly?
